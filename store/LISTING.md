**Description:**
Kovi makes daily vehicle walkaround checks quick for drivers and effortless for transport managers.

Drivers: scan the QR code in the cab, tick what's wrong (everything else defaults to OK), take a photo of any defect, finish. Works with no signal and sends when you're back online.
Managers: see today's checks at a glance, get instant alerts for defects and missed checks, track MOT, tax and service dates, handle defects to closure, and download PDF reports for audits.
Custom checklists for trucks, trailers, vans and plant. Tractor and trailer checked together.
Free 30 day trial for companies at the Kovi website. £1 per vehicle per week after.
This app is for existing Kovi customers: drivers sign in with the company code and a PIN from their manager.
