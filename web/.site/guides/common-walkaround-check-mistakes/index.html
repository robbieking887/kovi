8 common walkaround check mistakes

A check that does not catch anything is worse than none, because it gives false comfort.

Updated 1 November 2026 · 2 min read

Home › Guides › Common walkaround check mistakes
  1. Tick and flick. Ticking every box without looking. Fix: short lists, one section at a time, and a photo when something is wrong.
  2. Checks done at the end of the day. The check is meant to be before use. Fix: time-stamped records.
  3. No one reads them. Fix: a daily look at the Today screen.
  4. Defects never closed. Fix: assign, fix, record.
  5. One list for every vehicle. Fix: templates by vehicle type.
  6. Paper that gets lost, wet or illegible. Fix: digital records.
  7. No trailer check. Fix: add the trailer section.
  8. Blaming reporters. Fix: thank them.
