Safety guide

Breakdown safety for lorry and van drivers

Breakdowns on fast roads are dangerous. The right steps in the first minute keep you alive.

Updated 1 November 2026 · 2 min read

Home › Guides › Breakdown safety

If you can, get off the road

Try to reach an exit, a services or a safe place if you can do so without making things worse. Follow current National Highways advice, because arrangements on different types of motorway vary.

If you must stop on the road

  1. Switch on hazard lights.
  2. Get everyone out of the vehicle by the safest door, away from traffic, and move well away from the carriageway and behind a barrier if there is one.
  3. Do not stand between the vehicle and traffic.
  4. Call for help and give your exact location.
  5. If you cannot get out safely and you are in a live lane, call 999 and follow instructions.

Do not

  • Do not try repairs in a live lane.
  • Do not put out a warning triangle on a motorway.
  • Do not sit in a vehicle that is stopped in a live lane if you can safely leave it.

Prevention

A large share of breakdowns have signs the day before. Daily checks, reported early, turn roadside breakdowns into yard repairs. See the cost of downtime.
