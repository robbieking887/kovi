Guides to daily vehicle checks

Practical help for fleet operators, transport managers and drivers in Great Britain.

Home › Guides

These guides explain what a good daily check looks like, what to record, how to stay safe and what the rules expect. Start with 12 things fleet operators often forget. They are general information, not legal advice. Rules change, so check the latest guidance from DVSA and GOV.UK for your situation.
