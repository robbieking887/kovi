Trailer walkaround check guide

The trailer is a vehicle in its own right. Check it on its own merits, and again every time you couple up.

Updated 1 November 2026 · 2 min read

Home › Guides › Trailer walkaround check guide

Before coupling

  • Landing legs and any damage to the trailer front
  • Kingpin and fifth wheel plate in good condition and lubricated
  • Air lines and electrical connectors undamaged

After coupling

  • Coupling locked, with a tug test
  • Air lines connected and no leaks, brakes work
  • Electrical connection secure and all lights work
  • Landing legs fully raised and handle secured

Trailer walkround

  • Tyres and wheels, including nuts
  • Lights, reflectors and number plate
  • Body, doors, curtains and ropes
  • Rear underrun bar and spray suppression
  • Load secure

Common faults

Lights not working after a new coupling, damaged air lines and worn tyres are the typical ones. Photograph and report each.

Recording it

In Kovi the trailer is added as a vehicle and the driver completes the trailer section of the check, so each trailer has its own history.
