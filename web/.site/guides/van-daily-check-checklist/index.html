Van daily check checklist

Two minutes before you pull away. Vans work hard and are often shared between drivers, so defects can go unnoticed for days.

Updated 1 November 2026 · 2 min read

Home › Guides › Van daily check checklist

Walk round

  • Tyres: tread, pressure by sight, no bulges or cuts
  • Wheels: nuts present and tight
  • Lights: headlights, indicators, brake lights, hazards
  • Windscreen: clean, no cracks in the driver's view, wipers and washers work
  • Mirrors, number plates and body: undamaged and clean
  • Leaks under the vehicle

In the cab

  • Seat belt, horn, handbrake
  • Warning lights off after start
  • Fuel level and any mileage record

Load

  • Load secured, not above the weight limit, and not blocking the driver's view
  • Racking and bulkhead sound
  • Ladders and roof loads fixed

Paperwork

  • Licence, and any company fuel card or permits

Why employers record it

Employers have a general duty to keep work vehicles safe. A recorded daily check shows that drivers are asked to look at the vehicle and that defects get dealt with. See the van fleet page for how Kovi helps.
