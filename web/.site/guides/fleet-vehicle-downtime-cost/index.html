What does a day of vehicle downtime really cost?

Most fleets know the repair bill. Fewer know what a vehicle off the road costs per day.

Updated 1 November 2026 · 2 min read

Home › Guides › Cost of vehicle downtime

The simple formula

Cost of a day off the road = lost income + cost of cover + wasted costs + admin.

  • Lost income: what the vehicle would have earned or the work it would have done.
  • Cover: hire vehicle or sub-contractor costs.
  • Wasted costs: driver wages for a day with no work, parking, fixed costs.
  • Admin: phone calls, rebooking, apologising.

A worked example (illustrative numbers)

A tipper that earns £450 a day, costs £120 a day to hire a replacement and has a £30 admin cost: roughly £150 to £480 a day depending on whether you can cover or not. Three roadside breakdowns in a year might cost over a thousand pounds before the repair bill.
