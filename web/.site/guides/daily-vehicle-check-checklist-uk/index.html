Guide

Daily vehicle check checklist for UK lorries and large vehicles

Use this as a starting point. Add or remove items to suit your vehicles, and always follow your own safety rules.

Updated 1 November 2026 · 2 min read

Home › Guides › Daily vehicle check checklist

Cab and controls

  • Windscreen clean and undamaged, wipers and washers working
  • Mirrors and cameras clean and correctly adjusted
  • Horn, steering and handbrake working
  • Seat belt and seat secure
  • No dashboard warning lights after starting

Lights and signals

  • Headlights, dipped and main beam
  • Indicators and hazard lights
  • Brake lights and reverse light
  • Reflectors, side markers and number plate light

Wheels and tyres

  • Tyre condition, tread and pressure by sight
  • No cuts, bulges or exposed cords
  • Wheel nuts tight, with no rust trails or missing indicators
  • Spare wheel secure if carried

Body and security

  • Doors, tailgate and curtains secure
  • Mudguards and spray suppression fitted and undamaged
  • Number plates clean and legible
  • Load secure and within the weight limit

Fluids and leaks

  • Engine oil, coolant and screen wash topped up
  • Fuel and AdBlue caps fitted
  • No oil, fuel, coolant or air leaks under the vehicle

Trailer and coupling

  • Coupling secure, air lines and electrical connection fitted
  • Landing legs fully raised
  • Trailer lights, tyres and body checked

Paperwork

  • Driver licence, and CPC card if applicable
  • Tachograph card and enough paper, if used
  • Insurance and any permits

This list is a guide, not an official or exhaustive standard. Adapt it to your vehicles and check the latest DVSA guidance.

Questions

Is there an official checklist I must use?

There is no single mandatory form. DVSA publishes guidance on what to check. Your system must cover the points relevant to your vehicles.
