Safety guide

Vehicle security and fuel theft prevention

Theft costs fleets money and time. Simple habits make you a harder target.

Updated 1 November 2026 · 2 min read

Home › Guides › Vehicle security and fuel theft

Parking and yard

  • Park in a secure, well lit yard with a locked gate, and nose-in where you can.
  • Use CCTV and make it visible.
  • Do not leave vehicles at home with the load on unless it is safe.

On the vehicle

  • Locking fuel caps, and check they are in place and undamaged every day.
  • Lockable tool boxes and secured load areas.
  • Keys and fobs controlled, with a log of who has them.
  • Trailer security such as king pin locks.

Daily check angle

Look for forced locks, damaged caps, missing fuel, signs of entry into the cab or load area and missing tools. Report suspicious changes at once, with photos. Checks with a time and location help your insurer too.

Fuel monitoring

Note fuel levels and mileage in a check with a number item, so unusual use stands out.

General information, not legal advice or a substitute for official guidance. Check HSE, DVSA and GOV.UK for current requirements.
