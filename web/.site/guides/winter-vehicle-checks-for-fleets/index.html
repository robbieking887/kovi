Winter vehicle checks for fleets

Cold, wet and dark conditions find weak points fast. A few extra checks before the first frost save breakdowns.

Updated 1 November 2026 · 2 min read

Home › Guides › Winter vehicle checks

Before winter

  • Batteries tested, because cold weather finds weak ones.
  • Tyres with plenty of tread, correct pressure and no damage.
  • Antifreeze strength checked.
  • Wipers replaced if smearing, and screen wash with winter strength.
  • Heater, demister and window heating tested.
  • Lights all working and cleaned, because shorter days mean more dark driving.

Every cold morning

  • Clear all glass, mirrors and lights of ice and snow. Do not drive with only a peephole.
  • Clear roof snow, which can fall onto other drivers.
  • Check air tanks for water and the brakes for sticking.
  • AdBlue can freeze in severe cold, so know your vehicle's behaviour.
  • Allow extra time and extra distance.

Carry

A torch, warm clothing, a shovel or de-icer, a charged phone and a high-visibility vest.

Report quickly

Frost makes defects obvious: air leaks, sticking doors and dead lamps. Make sure drivers report them with a photo, and that the office can see them. See defect reporting.
