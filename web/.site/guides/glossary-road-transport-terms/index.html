Road transport glossary

The jargon in one place.

Updated 1 November 2026 · 2 min read

Home › Guides › Road transport glossary
  • AdBlue: a fluid used in modern diesel engines to cut emissions.
  • Annual test: the yearly roadworthiness test for goods vehicles and trailers over 3.5 tonnes.
  • CPC: Certificate of Professional Competence, either for drivers (Driver CPC) or transport managers.
  • Defect: a fault on the vehicle that affects safety, legality or operation.
  • DVLA: the agency for driver and vehicle licensing.
  • DVSA: the agency for vehicle standards, tests and enforcement.
  • Fifth wheel: the coupling plate on a tractor unit that the trailer kingpin locks into.
  • GVW: gross vehicle weight.
  • Kingpin: the pin under the front of a semi-trailer.
  • MOT: annual test for vehicles up to 3.5 tonnes.
  • OCRS: Operator Compliance Risk Score.
  • O-licence: operator licence for goods vehicles.
  • PMI: planned preventive maintenance inspection.
  • Prohibition: a DVSA order that stops a vehicle being used until it is fixed.
  • Plating certificate: a document showing a vehicle's or trailer's plated weights.
  • Rigid: a lorry with the body and cab on one chassis.
  • Tachograph: a device that records driving time, breaks and rest.
  • Tractor unit: the cab and engine that pulls a semi-trailer.
  • Traffic Commissioner: the regulator for operator licences.
  • Walkaround check: the driver's daily pre-use check of the vehicle.
