Daily: before the vehicle moves

Anything that makes the vehicle unsafe or unlawful quickly: lights, tyres, brakes feel, mirrors, windscreen, horn, load security, warning lights and visible leaks.

Weekly: short and routine

  • Tyre pressures with a gauge.
  • Oil, coolant, washer fluid and AdBlue levels, if not done daily.
  • Wipers and washers.
  • Body damage and number plates.

Monthly or by schedule

  • Battery and terminals.
  • Fire extinguisher and first aid kit in date.
  • Dates for MOT, tax, service and inspection. See reminders.

Do not skip the daily

Weekly checks add to the daily, they do not replace it. Planned inspections by a technician are separate. See PMI vs daily check.
