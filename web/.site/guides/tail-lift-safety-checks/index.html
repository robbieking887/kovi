Why tail lifts hurt people

Tail lifts cause crushed feet, falls from the platform and trapped hands. Most incidents involve a worn part nobody reported, or a platform used in a hurry.

Before you use it

  • Platform surface clean and not slippery. Anti-slip surface intact.
  • No cracks, bends or loose welds on the platform or arms.
  • Hydraulic hoses and rams dry, with no leaks or weeping.
  • Controls work smoothly and stop when released.
  • Warning markings and the safe working load plate still readable.
  • Safety flaps, edge markings or lights working where fitted.

Using it safely

  • Keep hands and feet clear of the gap between platform and vehicle.
  • Never exceed the safe working load.
  • Keep other people clear while the platform moves.
  • Do not ride the lift unless it is designed for it.
  • Park on firm, level ground.

Thorough examination

Lifting equipment used at work is usually subject to periodic thorough examination by a competent person under lifting regulations. Keep the reports. The daily check does not replace it. Read our PMI vs daily check guide for how the two fit together.

Questions

Does a tail lift need a daily check?

A visual check before use is good practice and helps spot damage early. Periodic thorough examination is a separate legal requirement for most lifting equipment at work.
