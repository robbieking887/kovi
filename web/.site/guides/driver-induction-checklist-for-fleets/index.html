Safety guide

New driver induction checklist

The first day sets the habits. Thirty minutes of induction saves a lot of trouble later.

Updated 1 November 2026 · 2 min read

Home › Guides › Driver induction checklist

Before they drive

  • Licence checked and recorded, plus CPC if needed.
  • Right to work and contract paperwork done.
  • Fit to drive: health, medication and eyesight questions as appropriate.

The vehicle

  • Walk round the vehicle together, pointing out the daily check items.
  • Show the controls, brakes, mirrors, cameras, load restraint kit and any special equipment.
  • Do the first daily check together, using the actual process.

The system

  • How to sign in and do the check (in Kovi: scan the QR code and enter the PIN).
  • How to report a defect, with a photo, and who will respond.
  • What to do if the vehicle is unsafe: stop, tell the office, do not drive.

Policies

  • Phone, drugs and alcohol, hours, speed and fatigue.
  • Accident and incident reporting steps.
  • Breakdown procedure, including safety on the motorway.

Sign-off

Record that the induction was done, by whom and when. Agency drivers need the same basics, especially on the first shift.
