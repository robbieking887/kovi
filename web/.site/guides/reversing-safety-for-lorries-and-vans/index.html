Reversing safety for lorries and vans

Many serious workplace transport incidents involve reversing. The best reverse is the one you avoid.

Updated 1 November 2026 · 2 min read

Home › Guides › Reversing safety

Design out the reverse

  • Plan one-way routes through yards and depots.
  • Use drive-through loading where possible.
  • Keep pedestrians and vehicles apart, with marked walkways and barriers.

When you must reverse

  • Use a trained banksman, in view of the driver, wearing hi-vis.
  • Agree signals before starting. If the driver loses sight of the banksman, stop.
  • Stop and get out to look if you are unsure.
  • Keep other people clear of the area.

Equipment

  • Reversing camera and monitor clean and working.
  • Audible reversing alarm or white noise alarm working.
  • Mirrors adjusted, clean and not cracked.
  • Proximity sensors working, if fitted.

Make these items part of the daily check, and photograph and report a dirty or broken camera as a defect.
