What heat does to vehicles

High temperatures raise tyre pressures and wear, stress cooling systems and batteries and make drivers tired faster.

Summer checks

  • Tyres: check pressures when cold. Look for cracks and bulges. Hot roads punish worn tyres.
  • Coolant: level between the marks, with no leaks and the cap secure.
  • Oil and other fluids topped up.
  • Battery: terminals clean and tight.
  • Air conditioning and fans working.
  • Refrigeration units on temperature, if you carry chilled goods. See the refrigerated checklist.
  • Load: heat-sensitive items kept out of direct sun where possible.

Driver welfare

  • Carry plenty of water.
  • Plan breaks in the shade where possible.
  • Watch for signs of heat exhaustion and tiredness.

Never carry on with a temperature warning showing. Pull over safely and report it. See tyre checks and winter checks for the cold side.
