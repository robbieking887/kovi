PMI vs daily walkaround check: what is the difference?

Both look for problems. They are done by different people, at different intervals, to a different depth.

Updated 1 November 2026 · 2 min read

Home › Guides › PMI vs daily walkaround check

Daily walkaround check

  • Who: the driver.
  • When: before the vehicle is used each day.
  • Depth: visual and simple function checks.
  • Aim: catch obvious defects before the vehicle goes out.

Planned preventive maintenance inspection (PMI)

  • Who: a competent technician, in a workshop.
  • When: at planned intervals that the operator sets, such as every 6 to 10 weeks for many goods vehicles.
  • Depth: detailed inspection, often with the vehicle raised.
  • Aim: find wear and developing faults.

They work together

The driver spots what changes day to day. The technician spots what is wearing. A defect reported by a driver can trigger an extra workshop visit, and a PMI can tell you what the drivers should watch.
