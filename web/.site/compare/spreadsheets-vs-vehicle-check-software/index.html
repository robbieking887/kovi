Spreadsheets vs vehicle check software

A spreadsheet is a clever way to store checks. It is a poor way to collect them.

Updated 1 November 2026 · 2 min read

Home › Compare › Spreadsheets vs software

Where spreadsheets work

Keeping a list of vehicles, MOT and tax dates, and a log of faults is a good fit for a spreadsheet, especially while you are small.

Where they break

  • Collecting checks. Drivers will not open a spreadsheet at 6am in a cab.
  • Photos. Awkward to attach and find.
  • Alerts. Nothing tells you a vehicle was not checked.
  • Trust. Anyone can edit a cell afterwards.
  • Time and place. Not recorded unless typed.

The middle route

Use software to collect checks and keep your fleet list in Kovi too. Dates and reminders are built in, and you can export everything if you still want a spreadsheet.
