Forklift daily check checklist

Operators should check a forklift before each shift. It takes a few minutes and catches the faults that cause accidents.

Updated 1 November 2026 · 2 min read

Home › Checklists › Forklift daily check

Visual

  • Forks: no cracks, bends or excessive wear, and heel not worn
  • Mast: no damage, chains in good condition and not slack
  • Carriage and backrest in good condition
  • Tyres or wheels damaged or worn
  • No leaks of oil, hydraulic fluid or fuel
  • Overhead guard and load backrest secure
  • Data plate legible

Operating

  • Seat belt or restraint working
  • Horn, lights, beacon and reversing alarm
  • Steering, brakes and handbrake
  • Lift, tilt and lower smoothly, with no drift
  • Attachments working
  • Gas bottle secure or battery connector and charge good

If it fails

Take it out of use, tag it and report it. Do not use a faulty truck.

Legal context

Work equipment must be maintained and fit for use, and lifting equipment needs periodic thorough examination. Pre-use checks and records are standard practice. Check HSE guidance for your site.
