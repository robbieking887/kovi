Pickup truck daily check checklist

Pickups carry tools, trailers and loads in rough conditions.

Updated 1 November 2026 · 2 min read

Download the printable PDF

Home › Checklists › Pickup truck

Walk round

  • Tyres including the spare
  • Wheel nuts
  • Lights, indicators and number plates
  • Windscreen and mirrors
  • Underbody damage and leaks

Load and towing

  • Load bed secured and load restrained
  • Tow hitch and electrical socket
  • Trailer lights and coupling if towing

Cab

  • Seat belt, horn, handbrake
  • Warning lights
  • Fuel level
