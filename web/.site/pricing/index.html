Questions

Is there a contract?

No. Remove your vehicles or delete your company whenever you like.

Do I pay for vehicles I am not using?

You pay for the weeks a vehicle is on your account. Remove a vehicle and it stops being charged.

Do drivers cost extra?

No. Drivers and office users are unlimited.

What payment methods do you take?

Card, through Stripe. Invoices are emailed to your billing address.
