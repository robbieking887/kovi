Why we built it

Kovi started inside a working lorry business. Paper sheets got lost, checks were ticked without looking and nobody could find last month's defect. The existing apps were priced per user or per year and built for bigger firms. We wanted something a small fleet could try this afternoon and afford without a meeting.

What we believe

  • A check should take about a minute for a clean vehicle.
  • Drivers should never need a password.
  • Pricing should be simple enough to say in one line.
  • Your data is yours. You can take it or delete it.

Based in the UK

Kovi is built in Scotland for UK fleets, with data held in London.
