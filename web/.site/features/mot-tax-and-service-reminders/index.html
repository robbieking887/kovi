Dates in one place

Add the MOT, tax and service due dates to each vehicle. Kovi shows what is coming up on the office screen and emails reminders ahead of the due date, so there is time to book it in.

Why fleets forget

Dates in a spreadsheet only work if someone opens the spreadsheet. A reminder in the inbox does not rely on memory. It is a small feature, but a missed MOT or tax date is one of the most avoidable problems a fleet has.

Same system as the checks

The reminders sit with the vehicle, next to its checks and defect history, so the office sees everything about a vehicle on one page.

Questions

Does Kovi look up the dates for me?

No. You enter the dates yourself and Kovi reminds you. This keeps it simple and works for any vehicle.
