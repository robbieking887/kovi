Security

Security and your data

Your check records include drivers and vehicles, so we treat them carefully.

Home › Security and data
  • Hosting: Google Cloud, London region.
  • Encryption in transit: the whole site is HTTPS only.
  • Passwords: stored hashed, never in plain text. Minimum length and common-password checks are enforced.
  • Drivers: PIN login with attempt limits and lockouts.
  • Sessions: owners can sign out everywhere, which immediately revokes access.
  • Audit log: sign-ins and sensitive changes are recorded for owners.
  • Separation: every company's data is isolated from other companies.
  • Payments: card details go straight to Stripe. We never see or store them.
  • Your control: owners can export everything and delete their company and data from Settings.

Read our privacy notice, terms and driver notice.
