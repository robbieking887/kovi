Questions

Do I need a card to start?

No. You get 30 days free with nothing to enter. We'll remind you before it ends, and you add a card only if you want to carry on.

What happens when the trial ends?

You get 7 more days to add a card. If you don't, Kovi pauses until you do. Nothing is deleted, and you can export all your data any time.

Do my drivers need to download an app?

No. It runs in the phone's browser, and they can add it to their home screen. A phone camera scans the QR code. We're also preparing iPhone and Android apps.

What if there's no signal in the yard?

The check is saved on the phone and sent automatically when it reconnects.

Is it right for the checks I have to do?

Kovi records daily walkaround checks with time, location, photos and driver, using checklists you control. You stay responsible for your own compliance, and your checklist should cover what your operator licence and vehicles require.

Where is my data kept?

On Google Cloud in London. You can export or delete everything yourself from Settings. See our privacy notice.

Can I cancel?

Any time. Remove your vehicles, or delete your company in Settings. There is no contract.
