3. Phone location, in plain terms

The Kovi app only reads a phone's location and compass while a check is open: from when the driver taps "Start check" until they submit or cancel it. It does not track a phone before or after that, or in the background. We store where the phone was at the start and end of the check, the furthest it moved from the vehicle during it, and how many sides of the vehicle the phone was carried past (0 to 4). We do not store a continuous trail. If your business turns on handover records, the app also reads the phone's location once, at the moment a handover is saved, so the record shows where the vehicle changed hands. The office sees this to help confirm that checks really took place.

Where data goes outside the UK, it is protected by a recognised safeguard, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, or the UK-US data bridge.

6. Cookies and storage on your device

Kovi does not use advertising or analytics cookies and does not track you across other sites. The app keeps your sign-in and, while you are offline, checks waiting to be sent in your browser's storage on your device. These are strictly necessary for the app to work, so there is no cookie banner. Signing out removes your sign-in; checks waiting to be sent stay until they upload.

7. Security

We protect data with encryption in transit and at rest, hashed passwords and PINs, separation between customers, private photo storage, sign-in attempt limits and backups. No system is perfectly secure; if there is a breach affecting you we will tell you as the law requires.
